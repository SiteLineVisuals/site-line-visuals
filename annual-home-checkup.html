ANNUAL HOME CHECKUP

YEARLY MAINTENANCE DOCUMENTATION. ONE CONTINUOUS HOME RECORD.

Site Line returns once a year to document visible maintenance needs, capture annual thermal reference images when conditions permit, record homeowner-reported concerns and warranty follow-up, and update the permanent Digital Home Record.

Exterior → Interior → Thermal → Crawlspace & Attic → Filters → Warranty Record → Shopping List

What the yearly visit includes

  • Exterior and interior maintenance checklist
  • Annual thermal reference capture when conditions permit
  • Documentation of safely accessible crawlspace and attic areas
  • Accessible filter replacement when the correct homeowner-approved filters are available
  • Photo and video record of submitted home, appliance and warranty concerns
  • Warranty contacts, service visits, receipts, repair status and resolution history
  • Personalized filter, consumable and maintenance shopping list

Simple link-based issue tracking

Use your property link to report a light, refrigerator, appliance, leak, damage, warranty concern or other home issue. Add a description and photos or video. The issue and its interaction history stay connected to the home record—no separate app required.

Every documented item is organized as Monitor, Routine maintenance, Repair recommended, Warranty follow-up or Specialist recommended.

Pricing

  • $599: standard annual visit
  • $399: first visit reserved with a qualifying Site Line construction package
  • $499 annually: following three renewals, price locked for continuous members
  • $2,249: five-year prepaid plan with price protection and transferable remaining term

Why reserve now: save $200 on the first visit, establish the starting maintenance and thermal record while the home is new, and schedule the visit 10–12 months after completion—before common one-year builder-warranty deadlines when applicable.

After the initial price lock, continuous-member increases are capped at 5% per year. Homes over 4,000 sq. ft., multiple structures, difficult access or extended travel require a written quote. Consumables, specialist services and repair work are additional.

Important service note

This service records observable conditions and maintenance information. It is not a code inspection, engineering service, destructive investigation, diagnosis or warranty determination. Formal inspection findings require an appropriately qualified inspector. Access, safety, weather and equipment conditions may limit portions of the visit.
