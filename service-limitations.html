Site Line Visuals provides visualization and construction documentation — not architectural, engineering, surveying, geotechnical, environmental, code-enforcement, or home inspection services — unless a separately qualified provider is identified in writing.

LiDAR 3D scanning capture and drone/thermal imagery document visible, accessible conditions at the time of capture. They are not a substitute for a licensed inspection, survey, or engineering evaluation, and do not guarantee the condition of concealed systems or exact boundaries/topography.

Availability of any specific service depends on the selected package, project stage, site access, weather, applicable regulations, and the approved written proposal.
