OUR SERVICES

SEE IT BEFORE IT’S BUILT. DOCUMENT IT WHILE IT’S BUILT.

Choose construction documentation on its own, or combine it with a blueprint-based 3D model, an on-site VR walkthrough, and visualization of your finished home.

If you already understand your home’s design—whether you’ve reviewed it in person or are building from a familiar plan—you can choose construction documentation without purchasing a 3D model or VR walkthrough.

If you want to experience your custom home before construction, we turn your blueprints into a detailed 3D model. Walk your future home at full scale on your lot to understand room sizes, sight lines, windows, stairs, and how the spaces connect before construction begins.

During construction, scheduled visits document selected stages using LiDAR 3D scanning, thermal imagery, drone imagery, and progress capture. Preserve a visual reference of framing, visible electrical and plumbing runs, HVAC systems, and other accessible areas before finished surfaces cover them.

For a clearer picture of the finished home, our Interior + Exterior Visualization add-on brings furniture, materials, colors, and finishes into your home visualization. Use our guided intake to share furniture photos, shopping links, and design inspiration.

Your captured construction stages become an organized Digital Home Record you can return to after the build—for maintenance, repairs, remodeling, and understanding your home.

Documentation Only: Construction Capture → Finished Home → Digital Home Record
Full Experience: Blueprint → 3D Model → Full-Scale VR on Your Lot → Construction Documentation → Finished Home → Digital Home Record

Thermal Imagery

Add temperature-pattern imagery to your home’s visual record.

Thermal imagery documents visible temperature patterns during selected construction stages. These images provide another reference alongside your construction photos and scans and may highlight areas that warrant further evaluation.

Thermal imagery does not establish the cause of a temperature difference and is not a moisture diagnosis or inspection report.

  • Stage-based thermal capture
  • Visual temperature-pattern documentation
  • Added to your Digital Home Record
See thermal example →

LiDAR 3D Scanning

Keep a view of what’s behind the finished surfaces.

LiDAR 3D scanning creates a visual digital record of accessible rooms, framing, mechanical spaces, crawlspaces, and selected construction stages. Return to the captured spaces after construction to review visible systems and their locations before they were covered.

The record can help you and your contractor plan maintenance, repairs, or remodeling by providing a reference of what was visible at the time of capture. Coverage depends on the selected stages and accessible areas.

  • Framing and visible systems before they are covered
  • Accessible crawlspaces and hard-to-see areas
  • A lasting 3D construction record
See LiDAR example →
