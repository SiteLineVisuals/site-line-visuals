NEW CONSTRUCTION PACKAGES

NO 3D DESIGN OR VR REQUIRED

Construction Documentation

$2,495
For tract-home buyers, builders, contractors and homeowners who already understand the finished design and want the construction documented.
  • Complimentary Site Verification outside the three-visit allowance
  • 3 scheduled construction-documentation visits
  • Interior underground plumbing capture when applicable
  • Combined framing, electrical, plumbing and HVAC rough-in documentation
  • Final interior and exterior thermal reference capture
  • Accessible crawlspace and attic documentation at final
  • Drone/site imagery when safe, legal and conditions permit
  • Organized permanent Digital Home Record
  • Eligible for Home Intelligence and Annual Home Checkup upgrades
Best For: Tract homes, repeat plans and builders who already provide a model home or their own visualization. Does not include blueprint-to-3D modeling, designed imagery, preconstruction VR or a guided VR walkthrough. Additional authorized construction visits are $350 each.

Level 1 - Visual Build Package

$3,495
For homeowners and builders who want to experience the home before construction and document the most important early stages.
  • Blueprint-to-3D model preparation
  • Interior and exterior walkthrough capability
  • Full-scale VR-ready lot experience
  • Up to 3 scheduled site visits
  • Construction documentation at selected milestones
  • Framing, visible mechanicals and utility documentation when applicable
  • Thermal documentation at the appropriate stage
  • Drone/progress imagery when conditions permit
  • Organized permanent project record
Best For: Essential visualization + milestone documentation. Standard base pricing applies to a residential project up to approximately 2,500 sq. ft.

Level 2 - Project Monitoring

$5,495
For homeowners, investors and remote clients who want continued visibility during construction.

Includes everything in Level 1, plus:

  • Up to 6 scheduled site visits
  • Expanded progress scanning
  • Additional milestone documentation
  • Thermal, drone, walkthrough and hidden-system capture at selected stages
  • Crawlspace documentation when applicable
  • Expanding construction memory archive
  • Remote project visibility
Best For: Continued oversight throughout the build

Level 3 - Active Build Monitoring

$7,495
For clients who want frequent documentation and a much more complete visual history of construction.

Includes everything in the visualization foundation, plus:

  • Up to 12 scheduled site visits
  • Frequent construction progress capture
  • 3D/LiDAR documentation
  • Thermal imaging at appropriate stages
  • Drone progress imagery
  • Framing, mechanical, utility and insulation documentation
  • Crawlspace and accessible attic documentation
  • Finish-stage and major milestone capture
  • Expanded permanent 3D Construction Record
Best For: High-visibility construction documentation

Level 4 - Full Visibility

STARTING AT $9,995
For luxury homes, large custom projects, multiple structures, complex sites or projects requiring a custom capture schedule.

Best For: Complex & Luxury Builds. Scope and price depend on size, duration, travel, access, capture frequency, production complexity, storage, and delivery.

ANNUAL HOME CHECKUP & MAINTENANCE RECORD

A scheduled yearly visit that adds maintenance observations, annual thermal reference images, accessible crawlspace and attic documentation, filter planning, homeowner-reported issues and warranty follow-up to the permanent Digital Home Record.

Final annual scope and price are confirmed in the written proposal based on home size, access, travel, and documentation requirements.

Homes over 4,000 sq. ft., multiple structures, difficult access or extended travel require a written quote. Consumables, specialist services and repair work are additional. Observational maintenance documentation only; not a formal inspection or diagnosis.

MR. & MRS. SMITH PROTECTION — CHANGE & APPROVAL RECORD

Construction changes should never depend on memory or a handshake. This proposed add-on creates an organized, timestamped record of significant requested changes, who requested them, supplied cost or schedule information, and the required owner and contractor approvals.

  • $795 per project: up to 15 qualifying documented changes
  • $49 each: additional documented changes
  • $1,295+: large custom, estate or unusually change-intensive projects

Draft preview only. Not active and not available for purchase or signing. Activation is subject to Idaho construction-counsel review, an approved change-order form and coordination with the governing owner-builder agreement. Site Line documents the record; it does not provide legal advice or determine whether a change is legally binding.

PRIVATE CONSTRUCTION INSPECTION

An optional private construction-quality review supported by Site Line documentation and performed or coordinated through a properly qualified, licensed or certified inspector as required for the project location.

  • Single construction-stage inspection: starting at $495
  • Three-stage construction inspection: starting at $1,495
