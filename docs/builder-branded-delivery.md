# Builder branded client delivery

Approved direction: keep the existing public website and shared intake. Builder branding leads on client-facing deliverables; a smaller Site Line Visuals production credit accompanies it. The contractor page demonstrates this with clearly labeled placeholder samples.

## Company setup
Collect company name, authorized logo file (SVG or transparent PNG preferred), brand colors, letterhead, client-facing contact details, approval contact, and permission to use branding. Associate the approved brand kit with each project before production. Do not infer the builder from an address or trade contractor.

## Delivery standards
- Construction photos: builder logo in a consistent corner; smaller "Brought to you by Site Line Visuals" credit; keep labels and construction evidence legible.
- Documents: builder letterhead, project identity and capture date/stage; SLV production credit in footer.
- 3D walkthrough and interior visualization presentations: builder branding on opening/cover and agreed presentation materials; clearly identify proposed renderings. Interactive viewer branding requires a separate implementation check.
- Marketing: agreed finished assets from authorized job imagery; retain master files at SLV; scope, revisions, usage and placement spending agreed in writing.
- Preserve original capture files. Brand delivery copies only; do not overwrite evidence or original metadata.
- Obtain approval of a first sample, then check every delivered photo/document/presentation against the approved brand kit, project identity and credit placement.
