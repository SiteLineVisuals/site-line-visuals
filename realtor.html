FEATURED UPGRADES

CRAWLSPACE & ATTIC 3D / LiDAR SCANNING

OPTIONAL REALTOR / EXISTING HOME ADD-ON

SEE THE SPACES MOST HOMEOWNERS NEVER SEE.

Many homeowners purchase a property without ever fully seeing the crawlspace or attic. Site Line Visuals can create a navigable 3D/LiDAR visual record of safe and accessible crawlspace and attic areas so the captured space can later be revisited digitally or in compatible VR.

This can provide a dated visual reference showing the location and visible condition of: Plumbing, Ductwork, Insulation, Framing, Mechanical components, Utilities, and Other accessible systems.

Documentation represents visible conditions at the time of capture and is not a home inspection, engineering evaluation or guarantee of concealed conditions.

VIRTUAL FURNITURE / STAGING

For occupied or vacant listings. Existing furniture and personal contents can be digitally removed and the space can be professionally restaged for presentation.

$350 to $1,500+, depending on the number of rooms, style, level of detail, and rendering complexity — confirmed in your written proposal.
