Home / Realtor / Existing Home

SHOW THE PROPERTY CLEARLY. GIVE BUYERS A RICHER WAY TO UNDERSTAND IT.

A separate visual-documentation and presentation path for listings, remote buyers, investment properties, cabins, short-term rentals, luxury homes, and existing homeowners — built around 3D capture, thermal imagery, aerial documentation, and accessible-space scanning, not just listing photos.

EXISTING HOME PACKAGES

Every package includes virtual decluttering and staging of selected interior photos, accessible crawlspace and attic scans, and a Branded Property Marketing Package: professionally edited property footage with your approved logo and branding, delivered as a finished video ready to share on your website, social media and listing channels.

Virtually staged photos are labeled as digitally edited; original photos are retained in the property record. Selected rooms and scan access are confirmed before capture.

Existing Home Visual Package

$1,495
Show buyers the home's possibilities and give them a clearer record of the property. Includes a 3D walkthrough, virtually staged interior photos, accessible crawlspace and attic scans, and a branded marketing video.
  • One coordinated property visit for the agreed interior and exterior capture
  • Interior LiDAR/3D walkthrough to explore the captured rooms and layout
  • Virtual decluttering of selected interior photos — digitally remove personal belongings and movable clutter
  • Virtual staging — add furniture and décor to those photos to help buyers picture the space
  • Crawlspace and attic scans of safely accessible areas, documenting visible systems and conditions
  • Delivered walkthrough for compatible desktop and VR viewing
  • Interior and exterior thermal documentation where appropriate
  • Drone/exterior/lot-context imagery when permitted
  • Organized visual property record with the captured imagery and walkthrough
  • Branded Property Marketing Package — edited property video with your approved logo and branding
Intended for standard homes under 3,500 sq. ft.

Premium Existing Home

$2,495
More information about the home and more ways to market it. Adds equipment and finish records, expanded thermal documentation and additional shareable marketing materials.

Includes everything above, plus:

  • Everything in the Visual Package: walkthrough, virtually staged photos, accessible crawlspace and attic scans, and branded marketing video
  • Expanded thermal imagery of accessible areas, organized by location
  • Photo record of selected equipment and appliances, including visible model and serial information
  • Additional finish and product documentation for future reference
  • Short edited clips from the property footage for social media
  • Selected branded still images for listing and website use
Intended for standard homes under 3,500 sq. ft.
