CONTRACTOR PARTNER PROGRAM

Offer clients a clearer way to understand and document the home while strengthening your own sales presentation.

A contractor can introduce Site Line Visuals during the estimate, design or preconstruction conversation as an optional client resource. Site Line then explains its services, confirms the selected scope and provides the visualization or documentation directly connected to the homeowner’s project.

HOW WE WORK SIDE BY SIDE

  • The contractor introduces Site Line as an optional visualization and documentation partner.
  • Site Line explains its packages, add-ons, schedule and deliverables to the homeowner.
  • The contractor remains responsible for construction means, methods, pricing, scheduling, trade coordination and project authority.
  • Site Line remains responsible for its approved visualization, capture, documentation and record-delivery scope.
  • Both parties communicate through the project’s established chain of command and keep the homeowner informed within their respective roles.

WHY IT HELPS THE CONTRACTOR

  • Differentiates the contractor’s sales presentation without requiring the contractor to operate the technology
  • Helps clients understand plans, spaces and selections before construction advances
  • Creates clearer visual references for project conversations
  • Helps reduce misunderstandings caused by assumptions or flat-plan interpretation
  • Provides organized documentation the contractor and homeowner can revisit

WHY THE PARTNERSHIP MATTERS

Example: A contractor is presenting a custom-home proposal, but the homeowners are nervous because they cannot fully picture the kitchen, great room and window layout from the plans. The contractor introduces Site Line Visuals as an optional part of the project experience.

The homeowners walk through the proposed home, identify questions before construction advances and enter the project with a clearer shared understanding. The contractor strengthens the sales presentation by offering a useful solution, while Site Line handles the approved visualization and documentation work.
