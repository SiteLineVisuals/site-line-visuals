Construction Documentation Only

$2,049
For builders, contractors and homeowners who already understand the finished design and want the construction documented.
  • 3 scheduled construction capture visits
  • Interior underground plumbing capture when applicable
  • Combined framing, electrical, plumbing and HVAC rough-in documentation
  • Final interior and exterior thermal reference capture
  • Attic and crawlspace scans and reporting where present and safely accessible
  • Drone/site imagery when safe, legal and conditions permit
  • Organized permanent Digital Home Record
  • Eligible for Home Intelligence and Annual Home Checkup upgrades
Best For: Established designs, repeat plans and builders who already provide a model home or their own visualization. Does not include blueprint-to-3D modeling, designed imagery, preconstruction VR or a guided VR walkthrough. Additional authorized construction capture visits are priced by proposal.

HOW WE KEEP YOUR COMPANY INFORMED

Agree on the contact, delivery channel and timing before the first visit.

PROJECT KICKOFF

Confirm scope, project contacts, brand kit, client permissions and capture stages. Identify who reviews deliverables and who receives the client handoff.

BEFORE & AFTER EACH VISIT

Coordinate access and stage readiness before capture. Include a visit update recording the capture date, stage, completed scope, access gaps and next action. Set timing in the proposal.

REVIEW & HANDOFF

Provide agreed review materials through the project’s chosen email/file-link channel. Prepare approved client deliverables with your logo, letterhead and a smaller SLV credit.

Visual Build, Extended and Detailed coverage include up to 3, 6 and 12 construction capture visits respectively. Site verification and the guided VR walkthrough are outside those capture visits. The communication plan includes kickoff, coordination and an update for each completed capture visit, and final handoff; these are touchpoints, not guaranteed separate calls. Documentation Only includes three construction capture visits; custom coverage follows the written proposal. Capture records are distinct from formal inspection reports, which require the separately agreed qualified-inspector service.
