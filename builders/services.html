OUR SERVICES

SEE IT BEFORE IT’S BUILT. DOCUMENT IT WHILE IT’S BUILT.

Site Line Visuals offers construction documentation by itself or combines it with advanced preconstruction visualization.

If you already know the design—such as a home design you have already reviewed in person—you can begin with construction documentation and skip 3D design and VR. Custom-home clients can begin with their blueprints, transform them into a detailed 3D model and experience the proposed home at full scale, where it belongs on your lot, before construction begins.

Once the build begins, Site Line can return at scheduled construction milestones to create an expanding visual record using LiDAR 3D scanning, thermal imaging, drone imagery, progress capture and detailed construction documentation. Important framing, utilities, mechanical systems and other areas can be documented before they disappear behind finished surfaces.

For clients who want to see beyond the construction plans, our high-end photorealistic visualization can bring interiors, exteriors, materials and finishes to life before the home is completed.

The result is one connected visual journey:

Documentation Only: Construction Capture → Finished Home → Digital Home Record
Full Experience: Blueprint → 3D Model → Full-Scale VR → Construction Documentation → Finished Home → Digital Home Record
