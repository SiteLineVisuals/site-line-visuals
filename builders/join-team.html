CONTRACTOR PARTNER INTEREST

CONTRACTOR PARTNER PROGRAM

Your company. Your client. Your brand. Site Line Visuals does the production behind your offering.

Offer visualization and construction documentation as part of your company’s client experience. Choose the SLV package and add-ons that fit the build, agree on the scope and schedule, and present the service to your client under your company’s brand. We handle the approved capture, production and organization behind the work.

YOUR PRICE. YOUR CLIENT RELATIONSHIP.

The contractor prices on this website are what your construction company pays Site Line Visuals. You decide what to charge your client and how to include the service in your offering. Your company remains the primary client contact; SLV coordinates through you and your approved project contacts.

WHAT YOUR CONSTRUCTION COMPANY GETS

  • A defined package, capture schedule and deliverable scope confirmed in a written proposal before work begins.
  • A structured intake for plans, project contacts, room choices and inspiration photos.
  • The agreed visualization, construction-stage documentation and selected add-ons produced by SLV.
  • Deliverables prepared with your authorized logo and letterhead, with a smaller “Brought to you by Site Line Visuals” credit, according to the approved branding scope.
  • Coordination at setup, the agreed capture stages and delivery checkpoints. Reporting frequency, delivery method and contractor review are confirmed for your project.

WHAT YOUR CLIENT RECEIVES

Your client receives the selected presentations, construction photos, records and other agreed deliverables under your company’s brand. Depending on the purchased scope, these can include a proposed-home walkthrough, documented construction stages, thermal reference imagery, or a Home Intelligence Record. Your company presents the work and controls the client relationship.

HOW WE WORK SIDE BY SIDE

  • You select the services and provide an authorized brand kit; we agree on scope, schedule and the first branding proof.
  • We coordinate access and capture timing through you and your approved contacts, then prepare the agreed records and presentations.
  • You receive the agreed contractor review before client delivery, with findings and concerns handled through the project’s established communication process.
  • You remain responsible for construction methods, construction pricing, scheduling, trade coordination and project authority.
  • SLV remains responsible for its approved visualization, capture, documentation and record-delivery work.

WHY THE PARTNERSHIP MATTERS

Example: Your clients want to understand the kitchen, great room and window choices before construction advances. You offer the selected visualization package as part of your proposal. SLV uses the supplied plans and intake preferences to produce the agreed presentation under your company’s brand.

As the build progresses, the selected capture stages create an organized reference your company and client can revisit. You offer a professional client experience while SLV handles the approved production work behind it.

Logo placement, letterhead, custom presentation materials and model plates are confirmed in the written scope. SLV prepares the approved branded deliverables; this does not require your company to operate our production tools.
