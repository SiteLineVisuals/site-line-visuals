ILLUSTRATIVE DEMONSTRATION · SAMPLE INFORMATION

SEE WHAT YOUR HOME RECORD INCLUDES

Your home has equipment to maintain, finishes to match, and construction details worth keeping. A Digital Home Record brings those references together so you can find them when you need them.

This fictional sample shows how information can be organized. It is not a delivered customer record. The contents of your record depend on your selected package, captured areas, and available product information.

EXPLORE A SAMPLE HOME RECORD ↓

SAMPLE HOME: CEDAR RIDGE

Explore each section below. Home Intelligence Record organizes core equipment, appliance, and finish information. Home Intelligence Plus adds deeper product documentation, warranty and manual organization, and a maintenance plan. Construction scans come from your selected documentation service.

Equipment & Appliances · Home Intelligence Record

Example: Laundry room washer

Location
Laundry room, north wall
Product reference
Illustrative washer; model DEMO-W100; serial SAMPLE-001. These identifiers are fictional.
Recorded information
Equipment category, manufacturer, model and serial number, accessible identification-label photo, and available installation or purchase reference.

How you use it: When a service company asks which washer you own, find its identification information without moving the appliance. Equipment and appliance entries can also connect to recorded filter or replacement references.

Actual entries use the information that can be captured or supplied. Missing labels or unavailable records are noted rather than guessed.

Finishes & Matching References · Home Intelligence Record

Example: Living room wall finish

Location
Living room walls
Finish reference
Sample warm-white paint, eggshell sheen; product label and color reference would be attached when supplied.
Related references
Room photos, available paint or flooring labels, finish selections, and supplier information provided for the project.

How you use it: Find the recorded color, sheen, or flooring product when planning a touch-up or replacement. A matching library keeps those references connected to the room where they belong.

Photos support the record, but lighting and screens affect appearance. Product labels and original selection information provide a stronger matching reference when available.

Warranties, Manuals & Product Details · Home Intelligence Plus

Example: Heating equipment document folder

  • Equipment identification and deeper available product information.
  • Owner’s manual and manufacturer care instructions, organized with the equipment entry.
  • Supplied warranty documents, purchase or installation references, and supplier contact information.
  • Related maintenance and replacement references for future use.

How you use it: Find the manual, service contact, and supplied warranty paperwork together when scheduling service. The record organizes available documents; it does not create or extend warranty coverage.

Potential rebates or tax incentives identified for documented appliances or heating equipment can be noted for review, with available supporting references. Eligibility and current program requirements must be confirmed separately.

Your Home’s Maintenance Plan · Home Intelligence Plus

Example: Heating filter task

Task
Check the recorded filter and replace it as directed by the equipment instructions and household conditions.
Replacement reference
Filter type and size captured from the installed equipment or supplied documentation.
Timing
Based on the manufacturer’s guidance for the actual equipment; no universal interval is assumed.
Tracking
Record completion date, supplies used, and notes for the next service.

How you use it: Prepare the right supplies and follow a plan tied to your home’s equipment. The plan can organize recurring care, consumables, and replacement references.

The plan is documentation. An Annual Home Health Scan or Expanded Annual Checkup & Maintenance is a separate service. Agreed routine maintenance tasks follow the purchased plan; the homeowner supplies consumables.

Construction Stages & Scans · Documentation Service

Example: Before-drywall reference

  • Capture date, construction stage, and documented room or area.
  • LiDAR 3D scanning and imagery of visible framing and accessible electrical, plumbing, or HVAC runs.
  • Related thermal references and aerial progress imagery where included and captured.
  • Notes identifying captured coverage and areas that were inaccessible.

How you use it: Return to the captured stage before planning a repair or remodel and review where visible systems were documented before the finishes covered them.

This demonstration contains no customer scan or live scan viewer. Explore the existing LiDAR 3D scanning example and thermal example to see the capture methods. Coverage reflects the selected visits and visible, accessible conditions at capture.

Closeout & Future Updates

Example: Finding a reference after move-in

Start with a room or equipment entry, then review its connected product details, finish references, and available documents. Keep the construction record alongside the finished-home information so the two can be consulted together.

When an annual documentation service is purchased, its captured observations can be added to the record for comparison over time. Repairs, specialist evaluations, and additional capture are scoped separately.

What matters: An organized record of what was captured and supplied, with clear references and practical uses—not assumed information about concealed conditions.
