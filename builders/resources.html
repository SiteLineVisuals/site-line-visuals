What do I need to provide?

Plans for preconstruction modeling, project contacts, location and schedule, plus photos or links showing your design preferences. Documentation-only and existing-home services use the applicable capture information.

How do I show my furniture and décor?

Share photos of furniture you own, pictures or shopping links for pieces you want, and your inspiration through the guided intake.

What is the Digital Home Record?

The organized scans, imagery and dated construction history included in your scope. Home Intelligence separately adds equipment, finish and maintenance information.

What if construction has started?

Share the current stage before paying. We confirm what is still accessible and whether standard documentation-only coverage or a custom quote fits your project.

How do I receive and access the work?

We confirm the delivery links, supported viewing method and file formats for your selected scope. We do not promise unlimited storage or universal device compatibility.

Can I change the design?

We distinguish production corrections from customer-requested changes. Changes affecting building plans require the appropriate project professionals and may change cost or timing.

See Full Details

Who completes the intake and coordinates visits?

Your company and approved client contacts provide plans and preferences. We agree on the access contact, stage readiness and review process at kickoff.

See Full Details

Is my company branding included?

Approved standard client deliverables carry your logo and branding with a smaller SLV credit. Custom branding work is quoted separately.

Who sets my client’s price?

You decide how to resell or include the service. The contractor prices shown are your costs from SLV.

What does the visualization add-on provide?

Levels 1–3 include the blueprint-based model and on-lot VR walkthrough. The visualization add-on adds a furnished online walkthrough, six interior images and four exterior images. Share furniture photos, shopping links and inspiration through the guided intake.
