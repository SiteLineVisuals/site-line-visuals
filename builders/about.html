About Site Line Visuals

Built from more than 25 years of general contracting experience in North Idaho, Site Line Visuals helps people understand their home before construction—and remember how it was built afterward.

Experience the Plan. Preserve the Build.

Flat plans can leave important questions about room size, flow, windows and everyday use. We turn supplied plans into a 3D model you can experience at full scale on your lot.

During construction, agreed capture visits preserve accessible framing and systems before finishes conceal them. The resulting Digital Home Record gives the project team and homeowner a visual reference to revisit.

Your Company. Your Client. Your Brand.

You offer the selected service to your clients. SLV handles the agreed visualization and documentation. Standard client deliverables carry your approved branding with a smaller SLV credit. You control your client relationship and resale price.
