Our Process

Choose your coverage. Share your plans after payment. Experience the home and preserve the build.

Online checkout is being connected. For now, our sales team can review your selections and arrange the next steps.

  1. Choose your package

    Select the coverage for your build, then add optional extras.

  2. Review and confirm fit

    Review your selections. We confirm ZIP, size and construction stage before payment. Custom and starting-price work requires a confirmed written quote.

  3. Pay in full

    Fixed-price purchases are paid in full. Payment and cancellation terms must be available before purchase.

  4. Complete your project intake

    After confirmed payment, use your authorized project link to share plans, contacts, photos and design preferences.

  5. Prepare and capture

    We confirm your delivery date after reviewing your plans and project details. Visualization packages include the on-lot VR experience; construction visits follow your agreed stages.

  6. Receive your Digital Home Record

    Receive the agreed visuals and dated construction records. Selected Home Intelligence services add organized ownership and maintenance information.

How We Work With Your Company

  • Confirm project contacts, brand kit and capture stages at kickoff.
  • Coordinate access and readiness before each visit.
  • Provide a dated capture update and agreed contractor review.
  • Deliver standard client materials under your approved branding.

Site verification and the initial on-lot VR walkthrough are separate from the 3/6/12 construction-visit allowances. Documentation Only includes three construction visits without an additional complimentary visit.

What You Receive

Your selected package identifies the model, walkthrough, captured images and records included. File formats, access method and any custom deliverables are confirmed for your project.

Technology With a Purpose

3D modeling and VR explain the proposed home. LiDAR 3D scanning, thermal imaging and drone imagery document agreed accessible stages and conditions.
