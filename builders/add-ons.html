Interior + Exterior Visualization — Starting at $3,499

What we offer: Plans-based 3D and interior/exterior visualization within the selected scope.

Your company gets: A presentation to review with your clients, carrying your approved company branding.

Your client receives: The agreed walkthrough and finished images showing the proposed home, clearly identified as design visuals.

Walk through your planned home online and see it styled your way.

Your package includes an online walkthrough, up to six interior rooms with one finished photorealistic image each, and four exterior images. We create your first interior look from the inspiration photos and décor preferences in your intake.

  • Alternate décor style — $250 per change. Try up to two additional styles. Each change is a flat fee confirmed in your proposal with no added-room décor surcharge.
  • Additional room or image — $120 each. An added room includes staging and one finished image. Another viewpoint of an included room or exterior is also $120. There is no second image charge for the first view of an added room.

WHAT A DÉCOR CHANGE INCLUDES

Try different furniture styles and placement, rugs, curtains, pillows, bedding, artwork, decorative accessories, wall paint colors and color palettes in your interior images. We keep the same room layout, built-in features and camera viewpoints so you can compare the looks.

Changes to walls, doors, windows, cabinetry, flooring or the building design are outside a décor change and require a separate scope and price.

HOW TO REQUEST A CHANGE

After reviewing your first images, email one complete request identifying your project and rooms, the new look you want, and anything you want to keep. Attach inspiration photos or screenshots and include any product links. Your Site Line contact will confirm the requested scope before production.

Home Intelligence — Your Home’s Knowledge Record

What we offer: Organized documentation of qualifying equipment, appliances, finishes and ownership information.

Your company gets: A professional closeout package under your company letterhead, with documented details organized for handoff.

Your client receives: The selected Home Intelligence record to help locate home information, manuals and documented maintenance needs.

Turn scattered home information into one organized ownership record.

Home Intelligence collects qualifying information about the home, its installed equipment, appliances, finishes and ownership needs. Instead of searching through drawers, emails and labels years later, the homeowner receives an organized record connected to the property.

HOME INTELLIGENCE RECORD — Starting at $1,149

  • Equipment and appliance register
  • Available manufacturer, model and serial information
  • Filter and consumable information
  • Core finish and matching-reference library
  • Organized closeout record for qualifying project information

HOME INTELLIGENCE PLUS — Starting at $1,749

  • Deeper product and finish documentation
  • Expanded warranty and manual organization
  • Detailed maintenance and consumables planning
  • Supplier information and additional quality review
  • Supporting rebate and tax-incentive paperwork organized alongside warranties and maintenance records

Both records include a review of potential government and utility rebates, tax credits and deductions for documented appliances, insulation, heating and cooling systems, and other qualifying home improvements. We record relevant programs, eligibility requirements, deadlines, application links and required documents. Availability and eligibility must be confirmed; savings are not guaranteed, and your tax professional confirms tax eligibility.

WHY HOME INTELLIGENCE PLUS MATTERS

Example: Several years after construction, a floor tile cracks, a cabinet door is damaged and an exterior finish needs touching up. The homeowner remembers the general colors but does not know the exact product names, finish codes, suppliers or original order information.

Home Intelligence Plus preserves deeper product, finish and supplier details so the homeowner and contractor have a stronger starting point for locating a matching replacement or the closest available alternative. The expanded maintenance and consumables plan also helps the owner understand what should be checked or replaced over time.

WHY HOME INTELLIGENCE MATTERS

Example: Five years after moving in, the dishwasher needs a replacement part and the HVAC system needs a new filter. The appliance labels are difficult to reach, the manuals are missing and the homeowner does not remember who supplied the equipment or which filter size to buy.

With Home Intelligence, the available manufacturer, model and serial information, manuals, warranty records, supplier references, filter information and maintenance schedule are organized in one property record. The homeowner can quickly find the right information instead of searching through drawers, old emails and equipment labels.

Important: the record is based on information and labels available to Site Line. Manufacturer instructions and qualified service providers control actual maintenance and safety decisions.

Documented Change Authorization & Owner Approval

This proposed add-on creates one clear record for construction changes: who requested the change, who has authority to approve it, what it may cost, how it may affect the schedule, whether the contractor accepted it and when every required approval was completed.

PROPOSED SERVICE OPTIONS

  • priced by proposal — up to 10 qualifying documented changes
  • priced by proposal — additional documented changes

WHAT THE RECORD IS INTENDED TO TRACK

  • The requested change, affected location and requesting person
  • Available plans, photos, documents and other supporting evidence
  • Contractor-supplied price, allowance, schedule and scope effects
  • Required owner and contractor approvals, including delegated authority
  • Revisions, cancellations, rejections, disputes, timestamps and final disposition
  • A permanent audit history connected to the authorized project record

WHY THIS PROTECTION MATTERS

Example: One owner asks the contractor to move the kitchen island and add electrical work. The second owner does not know about the request. Materials are ordered, the work begins and the price and completion date change. Later, the owners disagree about who approved the added cost.

With the proposed Mr. & Mrs. Smith process, the request is recorded before work proceeds, the contractor provides the expected price and schedule effect, and every owner whose approval is required accepts or rejects the change. The timestamped record shows exactly what was requested, what was approved and who authorized it.

READ THE PROPOSED PROTECTION RULE

DRAFT — NOT ACTIVE — PENDING IDAHO CONSTRUCTION COUNSEL REVIEW.

Before the project begins, every legal owner must be identified and the governing construction agreement must state who may request and approve changes. Unless that agreement or a separate signed authorization clearly grants one person sole authority, a change requiring owner approval will not be marked approved in the Site Line record until every required owner and the builder or contractor have provided the required written or electronic approval.

Each change record is intended to identify the requested change, affected location, requesting person, available supporting evidence, contractor-supplied price or allowance effect, expected schedule or scope effect, required approvals, timestamps, current status and final disposition.

No verbal request, text message, field conversation, payment, scheduling action or Site Line entry by itself changes the governing construction contract. Emergency work, safety issues, code-required corrections, allowances, credits, cancellations, disputed changes, delegation of authority and work begun before final approval must follow the attorney-approved form and the governing construction agreement.

When a change requires new Site Line capture, the record will flag SCOPE CHANGE — ADDITIONAL CAPTURE REVIEW REQUIRED. A new trip is not automatically free and does not automatically consume an existing package visit. Site Line will review available adaptive capacity and, when needed, propose a paid change-order visit.

Site Line’s role is documentation and record organization. Site Line does not set the contractor’s price, direct the work, give legal advice or decide whether a change is legally enforceable.

Legal-review gate: this preview cannot be purchased, signed or activated. Final language, forms and workflow must be reviewed by Idaho construction counsel and coordinated with the project’s governing construction agreement before release.

Private Construction Inspection

Your price from SLV: Three stages starting at $1,749; single stage starting at $599 · pre-enrollment

What we offer: Optional private review by a properly qualified inspector; activation requirements still apply.

Your company gets: The agreed stage report for contractor review, with findings attributed to the qualified inspector.

Your client receives: The authorized inspection report with your company presentation and the inspector’s required identity and credentials preserved.

Add qualified human construction review to the Site Line documentation record.

An extra qualified set of eyes never hurts on a construction project. This optional service combines organized Site Line visual evidence with a private construction-quality review performed or coordinated through an appropriately qualified, licensed or certified inspector as required for the project location. The inspector—not the capture technician or AI—holds authority over inspection findings and the released report.

WORKING SERVICE OPTIONS

  • Three-stage construction inspection & reporting — starting at $1,495. Includes foundation, framing and final, with a written report for each stage.
  • Single construction-stage inspection & report — starting at $495. Available on its own or to add a stage to the three-stage service.

Foundation → Framing → Final. Read the draft inspection checklist and service standards. The complete checklist will be confirmed after licensing review.

INTENDED DELIVERABLE

  • Defined inspection stage and written scope
  • Qualified-inspector review of the accessible and agreed project areas
  • Relevant Site Line photographs, scans or other approved visual evidence
  • Organized written report with observations, limitations and recommended follow-up where appropriate
  • Report retained with the authorized project record

WHY A PRIVATE CONSTRUCTION INSPECTION MATTERS

Example: Before insulation and drywall cover the work, the qualified inspector reviews the accessible project areas and Site Line visual record. The inspector identifies an exterior window-flashing detail that appears incomplete and a framed opening that should be compared with the approved plans.

The observations, supporting images and recommended follow-up are placed in an organized written report and provided to the homeowner and general contractor. Follow-up proceeds through the project’s established chain of command so the general contractor can coordinate the appropriate trade, correction or professional evaluation while the work is still accessible. The response and any completed correction can then be documented before the area is concealed.

Important: this is a private construction-quality service. It does not replace building-department inspections, engineering, architecture, surveying, code enforcement, specialty-trade certification or any legally required approval. AI may organize evidence and assist the review, but it does not replace the qualified inspector’s judgment.

Activation gate: enrollment and payment open only after Site Line confirms the qualified inspector, applicable jurisdictional requirements, credentials, insurance, inspection checklist, report template, customer agreement and capture-to-report workflow.
